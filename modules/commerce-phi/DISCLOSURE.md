# Commerce Phi disclosure contract

Generated commerce sites must clearly disclose monetized/affiliate relationships. A product recommendation may be ranked for relevance to the shopper's request; payment from a merchant must not be silently represented as independent ranking evidence.

A click is an outbound referral, not a sale. A conversion or commission becomes recorded as such only when an authoritative merchant or affiliate-network report supplies it.

Prices and availability are time-sensitive fields. The product adapter should retain an update timestamp when supplied and the UI must not present stale data as verified current data.

Payment-card details remain with merchant checkout unless a future payment module is deliberately designed and reviewed for that responsibility.
