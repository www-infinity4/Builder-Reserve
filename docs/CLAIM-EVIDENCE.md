# Claim evidence linking

Research seeds begin as structured ideas, not automatic facts. Each claim can receive its own evidence record.

Evidence may support, contradict, contextualize, or describe a method. The builder keeps contradictory evidence instead of silently replacing it.

A claim is not promoted merely because a URL exists. Promotion requires supporting evidence and at least one stronger evidence class such as a peer-reviewed experiment, standard, or government dataset. This rule is deliberately conservative and can be refined by domain-specific modules.

The original wording remains available for provenance while generated pages display the current evidence status.
