# Oxide computing research module

This seed lets generated research sites organize oxide-computing concepts without turning an engineering hypothesis into a verified device claim.

The initial Al2O3/B2O3 record includes TiO2, SiO2, Ga2O3 and iron oxides as **candidate** complementary materials. Mechanisms are tagged separately as established, candidate, hypothesis or unknown.

The builder must not invent stoichiometric ratios, switching voltages, vacancy concentrations, quantum confinement, fabrication recipes or performance numbers. Those values require cited experimental or computational evidence for the specific structure.

A future research pass can attach literature sources and promote individual claims only when the evidence supports them.
