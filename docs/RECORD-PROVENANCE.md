# Record provenance

Every reusable research, legal, conceptual, or user-context record should identify where it came from.

The builder must not silently turn a user-supplied statement into an externally verified fact. It should retain the original date when one exists, note any structuring or summarization, and keep verification status separate from origin.

This also works in the other direction: builder-generated analysis must not be attributed to the user.

Suggested visible badges are **User supplied**, **External source**, **Builder generated**, **Verified filing**, and **System record**, accompanied by a separate verification state.
